# Lesson 04 — Tank and Pump System

A basic environmental engineering CAD exercise connecting a wastewater tank with a pump and piping system.

## Drawing information

- Tank diameter: Ø20,000 mm
- Inlet pipe diameter: Ø500 mm
- Pump outer diameter: Ø1,000 mm
- Pump inner diameter: Ø500 mm
- Tank center: (0,0)
- Pump center: (25,000,0)
- Drawing units: mm
- CAD software: AutoCAD 2026

## Topics practiced

- Plan-view engineering drawing
- Geometric positioning
- Object and center layers
- MOVE
- COPY
- OFFSET
- TRIM
- EXTEND
- MIRROR
- ROTATE
- Dimensioning
